Matcha Green Tea Mochi Bites

These vibrant green mochi bites burst with matcha flavor and crispy rice crackles. Perfect for Instagram-worthy snackables with a playful twist of sweet and crunchy textures.

Prep: 20 minCook: 15 minServes: 6Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup matcha powder
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter (melted)
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup crispy rice crackers (crushed)
  • 1/4 cup red bean paste (for filling)

Instructions

1

Whisk matcha powder with 2 tbsp milk until smooth. Set aside.

2

In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, and 1/4 cup crushed rice crackers. Add melted butter, egg, and vanilla. Stir until dough forms.

3

Roll dough into 1-inch balls, press a spoonful of red bean paste into each, then seal tightly.

4

Place mochi in air fryer basket, spray with oil, and cook at 350°F (175°C) for 8-10 minutes until golden.

5

Serve immediately with additional rice crackers and matcha powder for garnish.
